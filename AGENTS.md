# AGENTS.md

This repository is a BMad-style story-writing workspace for the serialized novel under `books/dream_of_boys/`.

## What to trust
- Use [docs/story-pipeline-guide.md](docs/story-pipeline-guide.md) for the overall workflow.
- Treat [books/dream_of_boys/story-bible.md](books/dream_of_boys/story-bible.md), [books/dream_of_boys/chapter-outline.md](books/dream_of_boys/chapter-outline.md), and [books/dream_of_boys/rules.md](books/dream_of_boys/rules.md) as the canonical story sources.
- Chapter drafts live in [books/dream_of_boys/chapter/](books/dream_of_boys/chapter/).

## How to work here
- Keep changes focused on the story pipeline artifacts and their supporting docs.
- Prefer linking to existing docs instead of repeating their contents.
- Preserve the established voice, naming, and chapter numbering already present in the story files.

## Story constraints to preserve
- Write for audio-first delivery: smooth, readable prose with natural dialogue.
- Keep content community-safe and avoid profanity unless the user explicitly asks for it.
- Respect the dream logic and the emotional arc from oppressive to luminous.
- Do not state the real-world source of a personality directly in the story text.
- Keep Aki, Ichi, and Sarah consistent with the fixed character rules until the planned merge.
- Keep Sarah at 15 and use metaphor, not direct depiction, for the sister's death.

## Before drafting or editing chapters
- Check the outline first, then the rules, then the relevant chapter draft.
- Make sure any new chapter content matches the chapter count and sequence in the outline.
- If a change affects continuity, update the outline or rules before expanding the chapter text.
